Imagine the following situation. You work for a big puzzle company and your boss gives you the following task. 
You have to create 1000 puzzles. The rules of the assignment are as follows: 
- The player is given two 3-letter English words. A start word and a finish word. The game is very simple. 
- The player has to get from the start word to the target word by changing one letter at each step, and by getting an existing, meaningful English word. 

Let's look at an examples: 
Starting word: FIT Target word: CAT Possible solution: FIT-FAT-CAT 
Starting word: ADD Target word: AFT Possible solution: ADD-AID-AIR-AIT-AFT 
Starting word: PAP Target word: FEN Possible solution: PAP-PEP-PEN-FEN 
Starting word: FIG Target word: VAT Possible solution: FIG-FIT-FAT-VAT


Now it's your turn. Make 100 of these puzzles and give me the solution to each one. 
Please when you give me the puzzles and their answer don't give any other word, 
just the ones that need for the puzzles and their solve.
