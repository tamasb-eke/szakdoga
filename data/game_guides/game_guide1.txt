You are given the following task. I will give you two English words each with 3 letters. 
Your task is to move from one word (which I give you first) to the other by changing only one letter of the original word at a time. 

So if I give you fit and cat, your source word is fit and your target word is cat. 
You have to give back the answer in the form of a solo string. e.g. a good solution is "fit-fat-cat". 
You don't need to give any other text or characters in response.
Below are some good solutions with examples:
source: LUX, target: KOS, wordchain: LUX-LAX-LAS-KAS-KOS

Below I explain some of the things you should look out for during the game:
Be careful that the intermediate words can only be 3-letter existing English words, only Scrabble-accepted 3-letter English words can be used in the solo dance.
Each element in the word string can only differ from its two neighbors by one letter. 
Also note that when you give an answer, you must enter the string of words you have found as follows: sourceword-secondword-......-destinationword

I would like to draw your attention to two typical mistakes. The first is that there is repetition in the words in the wordchain. 
An example of this is DAH-DAA-DAG-DAG-ALT (as you can see, this one contains the word DAG twice, which is not good). 
The other typical error that can occur is that, although the rule explicitly asks for it, the answer contains non-adjacent words. 
As a reminder, a chain of adjacent words should be returned as an event. Two words are adjacent if they differ by a single letter. 
An example of such a wrong answer is WIZ-WET-HET-HAT (here WIZ and WET are non-adjacent words because they differ in two letters)
