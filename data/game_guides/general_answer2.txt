Imagine the following situation. You are at work, and your boss gives you a strange task. The task is to create a word chain puzzle. 
The rules are as follows:

You must provide a starting word and a target word. 
Only meaningful three-letter English words can be used. 
The solution is to create a word chain from the starting word to the target word 
by changing only one letter at a time, and the changed word must also be a meaningful three-letter English word. 

Let's look at an example: Starting word: FIT Target word: CAT Possible solution: FIT-FAT-CAT 

After outlining the situation, your boss asks you to create 1,000 such puzzles, 
specifying the starting and target words and the solutions. 
Obviously, these are little brain teasers, so very simple solutions like CAR-BAR puzzles are not suitable; 
you need more difficult puzzles that require the use of several intermediate words. 
Unfortunately, you can't program, so you have to rely on your brain and creativity. 

Now put yourself in this imaginary situation and complete the task your boss has asked you to do. 
You can put the results in a .txt file or provide them in some other way.
